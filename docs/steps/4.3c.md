# Step 4.3c: moving steps by drag and drop (C30)

Branch `feature/step-drag-drop`, based on `refactor/refactor` (e2f9532, all of phase 1 to 4 merged). Serves Niels'
C-test finding C30 of 17 Nov ("steps should be draggable, also between stages"). Mockup with his answers:
https://claude.ai/artifact/TEMxecxCKvMZK2kAciR9KD ("Reihenfolge und Drag and Drop", its C30 part; the C31 part, one shared
order on the board, is separate, later work).

## What Niels decided (2026-11-24, on the mockup)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Lift | A **grip** handle with the mouse; **press and hold** on the iPhone (350 ms); **Space** and the **⋯ menu** without dragging ("In Etappe 2 verschieben"). |
| Waiting steps | A step that "wartet auf …" **stays draggable**. If the new place contradicts the stage order, the link is let go and the toast offers **„Rückgängig“**; undo restores the place **and** the link. |
| Long distances | While dragging, the stages are reachable from a **dock** at the bottom, so a far stage needs no scrolling. |
| Arrows | The ∧ ∨ arrows are **gone**; the menu and the keyboard replace them. |
| Also | Dropping on **„Ohne Etappe“** works. Every move is undoable (toast „Rückgängig“). Keyboard: Space lifts, ↑ ↓ change the place, Page ↑ ↓ the stage, Space drops, Esc cancels, announced in a live region. |

## Data and migration

None. `tasks.stage_id`, `tasks.waits_on_id` and `tasks.sort_order` (4.3a) carry everything; no new column, no
migration, nothing dropped. A move only rewrites `sort_order` (and `stage_id`, `waits_on_id` where it must) of open steps
of one project or group.

## How it works

**Server: one validated action.** `App\Livewire\Concerns\ManagesStepMoves::moveStep(id, to, position)` is the only entry
point (project page and group page). `to` is a lane key: `none` or `stage-ID`; `position` is the place among that
stage's open steps counted **without the step itself**, `null` = the end. It goes to `ProjectEditor::place()`:

- **Ownership**: the step is resolved through the page (user, project or group, still open), the stage through the
  project; another user's id, a stage of another project, a finished step or a bad key is a calm "Das gibt es nicht
  mehr", nothing changes. The service refuses a stage of another project and a stage on a group step as well.
- **Stable positions**: the whole order of the project (or group) is read once under a lock; the target stage is then
  numbered 0..n-1 again. Two quick moves queue up and each sees the other's result (SQLite has no row locks; MySQL does).
- **Idempotent**: a step that already sits there is left alone (`StepMove::$changed` is false): no write, no toast, the
  previous undo stays.
- **"wartet auf"**: after a change of stage, every open link of this step, or to it, whose waiting side now sits in an
  **earlier stage** than the step it waits on is let go in the same transaction (it would wait for something that cannot
  start before it is done). "Ohne Etappe", the same stage and finished blockers never contradict.
- **Undo**: the page keeps `{kind: move, id, stage, index, cleared}` (`#[Locked]`). `ProjectEditor::putBack()` moves the
  step back to its old stage and place and sets the let-go links again, except where someone changed that link since
  (not overwritten) or it would close a loop.

`ProjectEditor::move()` (one place up or down) and `setStage()` (end of a stage) are gone; the menu calls `moveStep`.

**Page.** Every stage list is a lane (`ul[data-lane]`, also when empty, with a hint "Leer. Zieh einen Schritt hierher."),
every step a row with `data-step`, `data-title`, `data-list` and `data-waits-on` (the open step it waits on). Groups have
one lane (`none`). The row has a grip button (`aria-describedby` points at a short keyboard hint) and a ⋯ menu:
**Ganz nach oben / unten**, **In Etappe N verschieben**, **Ohne Etappe**, and "Wartet auf" for projects. The menu is a
bottom sheet below 720 px and a popover from there; every item is a plain server call.

**Script** (`resources/js/step-drag.js`, Alpine component `stepDrag`; `step-order.js` holds the pure rules):

- *Mouse*: drag the grip (starts after 4 px). *Touch and pen*: press and hold 350 ms, on the grip or anywhere on the step;
  a swipe (more than 9 px before the hold) is a scroll and lifts nothing, also over the grip. *Keyboard*: as above.
- While lifted: a ghost follows the pointer, a dashed slot shows the place, the lane it lands in glows, a bar at the top says
  what the drop does (stage and place, the waiting that goes, what comes onto or leaves the board), a dock at the bottom
  lists every stage with its count. Dropping on a dock chip puts the step at the end of that stage. Near the top and
  bottom edge the page scrolls (not over the dock). Every spot of the steps column belongs to a place (the nearest
  stage, between its steps by their middles), so there is no dead zone; letting go outside the column, over the notes,
  or pressing Esc cancels.
- On the drop the step is at its new place **at once** and shows a loading state (`aria-busy`, faded) while the request
  runs. A **server failure** (offline, 500, expired session) puts the step back and shows one plain line with
  "Schliessen"; Livewire's own error dialog and the native "page expired" confirm are suppressed for this call.
- A server render in the middle of a drag (another save) puts the slot and the lifted state back where they were.
- Keyboard announcements follow the mockup ("„…“ angehoben. …", "Etappe 2 · Server öffnen · Platz 2 von 3. Wartet dann auf
  Etappe 1.", "Schon ganz oben.", "Abgebrochen. …"); Tab or a click away cancels a lift.
- Reduced motion: no tilt or scale on the ghost, no sliding of the other steps, the flash after a save is a static tint.

## Decisions Niels should check

1. **Dock, not folding.** The brief said the other stages "collapse to a dock". Built: a dock bar with every stage and its
   count; the stages themselves stay open (the mockup's "Zuklappen" is not built).
2. **A drop on a dock chip goes to the end of that stage.**
3. **Touch lifts only by long press**, also on the grip, so a thumb that scrolls over the grip column never lifts a step.
   (The mockup also let a touch drag the grip at once.)
4. **Only the stage order contradicts a link.** The order inside one stage does not; "Ohne Etappe" never does. Both
   directions are covered: moving the waiting step to an earlier stage than its blocker, or moving the blocker to a later
   stage than a step that waits on it.
5. **A move that changes nothing says nothing** and keeps the earlier toast and its undo.
6. **Groups got the same** (one list, no stages, no dock): they shared the arrows, so without it they could not be
   reordered any more.
7. **Releasing over the notes column cancels** instead of dropping into the stage at the same height.
8. The bar over a drag names what comes **Aufs Board / Vom Board**; the browser computes it with the same two rules as
   `NextSteps` (current stage first, To-dos and Tasks only, not waiting on an open step). The server stays the authority and the
   toast after the drop is the server's.
9. **Not checked on a real iPhone** (press and hold, scrolling, the sheet): emulated touch only.
