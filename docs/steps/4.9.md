## What you can do now

For a To-do, Task, Inbox item and an idea in Später, in both layouts and on the Ideen cards:

- **Bearbeiten:** title, list, size, hard and soft date, area, first step ("Wo anfangen?" in Später), notes. One
  sheet: a card in the middle of the screen on the desktop, a bottom sheet on the phone. Enter in the title saves,
  Esc closes, the buttons stay at the bottom while the fields scroll.
- **Verschieben nach:** To-dos, Tasks, Später, Inbox (every list except the one it is in), one tap, no sheet.
- **Löschen:** no question. The item goes and the toast says "«…» gelöscht. Rückgängig". The same toast follows
  every save and every move, and "Rückgängig" puts the item back exactly (fields, place in the list and in the
  board order, "today").

Where: the ⋯ button on every row of the lists (Inbox, To-dos, Tasks, Später, the „Wichtig" band, the list sheets of
the phone, the peek panel in a session) and on every Ideen card. On the desktop it appears on hover and on keyboard
focus, next to the flag; on touch it is always there. The menu is a bottom sheet below 720 px and a popover from
there (positioned from the button, so a scrolling panel never clips it); ↑ ↓ move, Esc closes and gives the focus back.

## How it works

| Piece | What |
|---|---|
| `tasks.deleted_at` | One additive, nullable migration (`2026_11_25_000001`). **Task is now soft deletable.** `down()` only drops the column. Nothing is rewritten. |
| `App\Services\Planning\ItemEditor` | `update()` and `move()` (a move is an edit of `list` only), `delete()`, `restore()`, `undo()`, `values()`. All rules live here; the writes go through `TaskMutator`, like the REST API, MCP and the sort. |
| `App\Livewire\Shell\ItemEditor` | One component per page (the board and the Ideen page mount it). Listens for `item-edit`, `item-move`, `item-delete` (sent by the menu), owns the sheet and the toast, dispatches `items-changed`, which `Board` and `Ideas` listen for. |
| `shell/item-menu.blade.php` | The ⋯ menu, shared by the board rows (`shell/item.blade.php`) and the Ideen cards. |
| `livewire/shell/item-editor.blade.php` | The sheet (it reuses `shell/sort-details.blade.php` for dates, area and first step: CONVENTIONS 6) and the toast. |
| `ActionRegistry` | Three new actions: `edit`, `move`, `delete` (the 15th to 17th). A11 checks an entry point for each in Werkbank and Stapel, board and session. The Ideen page has one card for both layouts, so its entries carry no layout (`<x-shell.action>` now accepts that). |

## Rules

1. **Delete is a soft delete.** The row stays with its day plans, parts and links, so "Rückgängig" is lossless. The readers that start from a child table (day planner, split parts, data export, MCP) use `whereHas('task')`, which hides a deleted task; the suite passes with it.
   The old paths keep their real delete and now say so with `forceDelete()`: REST `DELETE /api/tasks/{id}`, the MCP
   tool `delete_task`, the old board, the capture undo, the repeat successor and the note undo. Their behaviour did not change.
2. **The task of a running session cannot be deleted** ("Das läuft gerade in deiner Session. Beende die Session
   zuerst."). Editing and moving it work.
3. **A date in the past is only refused when it is changed.** An overdue task can still be renamed.
4. **A move goes to the bottom** of the new list and of the shared board order (4.8), like a new arrival. An edit that
   keeps the list keeps the place.
5. **Später has no hard date** (as in the sort): moving there drops it; the soft date stays. Moving out of To-dos and
   Tasks clears „heute".
6. **Everything is checked on the server:** title 1 to 500 characters, notes up to 5000, list, size, dates, area of the
   user, first step up to 255. Messages are per field, in German and English, never an exception text.
7. **Calm failures:** an item that is gone or not yours says "Den Eintrag gibt es nicht mehr. Die Liste ist
   aktualisiert." and the lists refresh; a failed write says "Das hat nicht geklappt. Versuch es gleich nochmal."
   (the exception is reported, not shown). The save button says "Speichert …" and is disabled while it runs.
8. `undo` and the id of the item in the sheet are `#[Locked]` Livewire properties: the browser cannot set them.

## Decisions for Niels (please check)

- **Soft delete in the database** (instead of keeping a copy in the toast). A copy would lose the day plans, parts and
  links of the task. Deleted rows are **kept** (like deleted week-plan categories since 4.1); there is no trash view and
  no purge job. Say if you want a purge after 30 days.
- **No separate "move" dialog:** moving is in the menu, as one tap, and in the sheet as list chips.
- **Sizes can be taken away** (tap the chosen chip again). A To-do without size counts as Quick, a Task as Short.
- **Inbox is a target of "Verschieben"** ("decide later again"). It cannot be an unsorted list for an item with a size:
  the sheet hides the size for the Inbox.
- **Items inside the To-do session card have no ⋯ yet** (the card is one block with a start button; its rows are not
  `item` rows). Edit them from the To-dos list sheet. Project and group steps keep their own menu (4.3c).
- **The toast stays 10 s** and holds one undo at a time: a new change replaces the earlier undo.
- **Done items are not editable** here (they are not on the board).
- **Notes are plain text in the sheet**; the old app showed them as Markdown, that still works on the old screens.
