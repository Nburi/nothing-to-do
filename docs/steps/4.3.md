Branch `feature/projects-next-step`, based on `feature/free-time-ideas` (PR #21). Serves R6, risk F10
("a project turns into a wall of tasks"). Preview with Niels' picks: https://claude.ai/artifact/44vWRxyDcbJbkbr3YgCTPR

## What Niels decided (2026-11-17, preview rounds 1 and 2 plus brainstorming)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Groups | **Stay** (not folded into projects). Small, **one list** (To-dos or Tasks). The board shows the **next two**; progress over the whole group. |
| Projects | Bigger, steps in Inbox, To-dos and Tasks. The board shows the **next two** steps that wait on nothing. |
| Dependencies | Mostly **chunks**: a project has **stages** ("Etappen"); stage 2 waits on stage 1. For the rare single case a step can **wait on one other step** ("wartet auf …"). No diagram. |
| Roadmap | The stages in order, each with an **optional target date**. |
| Progress bar | Projects: **current stage only** (some projects never end); no stages = no bar. Groups: whole group. |
| Board card | Like the old app's group card: header with name, count and arrow to its page, thin bar, two steps inside, "+N weitere". Projects add their current stage (`Bugs fixen · bis 30.11.`). |
| Suggestion | Both visible steps of a project or group **may be suggested**; hidden ones never. |
| Empty projects | **Rest** as chips at the bottom of the Projekte page, not on the board. |
| Old fields | Notes (`brainstorm`) stay and come back as note cards in **4.3b**; `external_url` and `deadline` stay in the DB and the export, not shown. |
| Mixed old groups | A group whose open tasks sit in more than one list **becomes a project** at the switch. |
| Split | **4.3a** (this step): groups, projects, stages, next two. **4.3b** (next): note cards ("Lose" pile, drag onto a stage, → step) and **shared** `#tags`. |

## How it works

- `App\Services\Projects\NextSteps` computes one `Bundle` per project and group: the visible steps (≤ 2),
  the column it sits in, "+N", progress, current stage, and the ids it stands for / hides.
  - Project: next = open, in To-dos/Tasks, not waiting on an open task, in the **current stage** (first stage
    with any open step, in any list) or in no stage. Current stage first, then steps without a stage; each in
    the project's order. Inbox and Later steps stay in their own columns (Inbox to be sorted, Later = someday).
  - Group: its open tasks in order.
- Board (`Shell\Board`): `bundles` by column, loose `lists` without the bundled steps, `counts` = loose + visible
  steps. Cards come first in a column. The Stapel pile shows a card's first step on top.
- Pick (`Suggestions::rank`): hidden steps are excluded, so "Another" and the next task in a session only ever
  reach visible steps. Capacity and the crunch forecast still see **all** tasks (work that has to be done).
- Pages: `/board/projects` (overview), `/board/projects/{id}` (roadmap, stages, steps), `/board/groups/{id}`.
  Writes go through `ProjectEditor`. Done and "remove stage" are undoable; a stage removal never removes a step.
- Navigation: new registry action `projects` (A11): top bar tab (Werkbank), session rail link, fifth Stapel tab,
  session drawer link. The Ideen page got the fifth tab too.

## Decisions Niels should check

1. **Order inside a project** is the project's own order (↑/↓ on its page), not the board's To-dos-before-Tasks.
2. **Stage order** is the order you add them (no reordering of stages yet); dates are targets only and do not
   reorder or warn. Should a stage date become the soft deadline of its steps (feeds the early warning)? Not done.
3. **Inbox steps keep a stage open**: a stage is current until its last step is done, even one still in the Inbox.
4. **Steps without a stage** are always eligible (after the current stage's).
5. A **project step in Later** stays in Later (as an idea), never on the project card.
6. New steps on a project page go to To-dos or Tasks (select next to the field); group steps go to the group's list.
7. There is **no delete** for projects and groups yet (and no moving existing board tasks into one), same as the
   known shell gap "editing, moving and deleting single items". Empty ones simply rest.
8. The roadmap is a row of stages in order, not a time-scaled line (simpler, honest when dates are missing).
