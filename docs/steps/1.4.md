## The rules

**Capacity and load**
- Capacity of a day = its sessions in pomodoro blocks; today counts only the sessions that have not started.
- Load = the deadline work that `DeadlineFeasibility` plans into the day (earliest deadline first, quick tasks
  share a session, area links, soft gives way to hard) + work that fits nowhere, on its deadline day (today if
  the deadline already passed). Tasks without a deadline are not load.
- Overloaded = work is left over on that day, or load > capacity.

**Early warning (F7)**
- **On** at the first missing minute. The horizon is at least two weeks, so a crunch shows as soon as the plan sees it.
- **Off** only when nothing is late **and** the tightest deadline has at least one session (25 min) of room.
  In between it stays on: that is the hysteresis.
- **Notify** only for hard deadlines, at most once per local day, even if it switches off and on again.
  A soft-only crunch is active but quiet (never pushed, never red, F5). Soft turning hard is a new reason to notify.

**Soft slip (G2)**
- First miss: the soft deadline moves **7 days after the missed date, at least tomorrow**; `soft_slips = 1`
  ("still want this?").
- Second miss: the task goes to **Later**, the soft date is kept as a record. With a hard deadline the task
  stays where it is and only the soft date is dropped.
- Setting a new soft date, or taking a task back out of Later, starts the count over.

**Crunch decisions**
- Drop: the task moves to Later, nothing is deleted. Shrink: one size smaller (Block → Long → Short → Quick).
- Move and "ask for more time" are date edits the user makes; they need no service.

## Decisions I made (please check)

- **Roll-over length = 7 days** after the missed date (at least tomorrow). G2 says "rolls over once" without a length.
- **The warning switches on at any missing minute**, off at 25 minutes of room.
- **Load is deadline work only.** Undated tasks are not planned onto days, so they do not count.
- **Release day:** the first hourly run after the switch rolls every overdue soft date over once (to tomorrow for
  old ones). Nothing goes to Later on day one, because every counter starts at 0.
