# Step 4.8 · One shared board order and the calm board

Built 2026-11-24 on `feature/board-order`, stacked on `feature/todo-session` (PR #37, step 4.7). Closes C31
(order tasks, groups and projects freely, one order) and C29 (a board that stays calm with three or more
projects). The spec is Niels' answer to the mockup "Reihenfolge und Drag and Drop" (2026-11-24); no new mockup.

Decided by Niels, built as asked:

- **Variant A:** one slot per card, in one shared order of loose tasks, projects and groups.
- **„Wichtig" on = a band at the very top.** Engine order stays deadline, „Wichtig", board order (step 4.6).
- **Calm board:** at most two cards on the board at once, even with three or more projects.

## Data: one migration, additive

`2026_11_24_000001_add_board_position`: a nullable, indexed integer `board_position` on `tasks`, `projects` and
`task_groups`. It is **one number space per user** across the three tables (lower = further up). Nothing is
dropped, renamed or rewritten: `sort_order` keeps its meaning (the order of steps inside a project or group, the old
app's ordering, `set_task_order` of the API and MCP).

The backfill reproduces what the board showed before, so nobody sees a change until they drag something: per user
projects (by `sort_order`), then groups, then open loose tasks (marked first, then `sort_order`, then id). Finished
work and steps of a card get no number. `down()` drops the column again. Rows without a number sort last.
A new task, project or group goes to the bottom (`HasBoardPosition`, the `creating` event, so every way of creating
one is covered: the app, REST, MCP, repeating tasks, copies).

## Rules

1. **One slot per card.** A project or group is one slot however many steps it has; its steps follow it.
2. **A drop is local.** Only the entries of the dragged container swap places. Entries that were not in the container
   (the other columns, the cards beyond the fold) keep theirs.
3. **The resting cards follow the cards on show** when a drop contains a card, so a drop never changes which cards
   are awake. "Nach oben holen" puts a card at the very top; the lowest awake card then rests.
4. **A project with nothing to do right now has no card** and does not use up one of the two places.
5. **Loose tasks never rest**, only cards do.
6. **Engine:** unchanged order (deadline, „Wichtig", board order; Okay: board order before size, step 4.7). The board
   order is now the order of the shared space: a card's steps sit where the card sits.
7. Entries stay in their list: a card belongs to the column of its next step, a task changes list in the usual ways.

## Decisions I made (please check)

- **The band replaces the „Wichtig" reminder** (same two places: right of the timeline on the Werkbank, in the tray on
  the Stapel). It is not a full-width strip. Marked To-dos and Tasks leave their column; the Inbox keeps its marked
  tasks (the sort needs them). The „Wichtig" tab and rail entry hold every marked task as before.
- **Marked To-dos inside the To-do session card stay in the card**, not in the band (the card is the first thing of the
  To-dos column and marks lead it). A marked To-do that does not fit the 25 minutes sits in the band.
- **Marked steps of a project or group stay in their card** (as in step 4.6; the band holds loose tasks only).
- **New work goes to the bottom** (the mockup asked "oben oder unten?"; bottom is calm and what the old order did).
- **The fold is per column,** under the column its cards belong to. "Seit n Tagen" is not shown.
- **Touch:** no visible grip on the phone (a long press lifts, like steps in 4.3c); the grip is there for keyboard focus.
- **MCP `set_task_order` and the old board** still write `sort_order`; they do not move the new board order.
- **No undo toast.** A drop is its own undo (drag back).
- **To-dos and Tasks stay two columns:** an order across both columns was an open question; the order is one number
  space, but a drop only reorders within one container, and the engine still takes To-dos first.
