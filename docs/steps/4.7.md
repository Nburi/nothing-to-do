# Step 4.7 · To-do session and the Okay tie rule

Built 2026-11-24 on `feature/todo-session` (from `refactor/refactor`). The spec is Niels' answer to the mockup
"Reihenfolge und Drag and Drop" (2026-11-24). No migration.

## What changed

| Piece | What |
|---|---|
| `App\Planning\Pick\TodoBundler` | Pure. Fills one 25 minute session from To-do picks that are already in priority order: the most urgent always goes in, then the next ones that still fit; one that does not fit is skipped. |
| `Suggestions::todoSession()` | Ranks the loose To-dos at Okay (deadline work first, then „Wichtig", then the board order) and bundles them. Returns a `TodoSession` (tasks, picks, minutes, how many wait). |

## Rules

1. The day's To-dos are bundled into **one** session of at most 25 minutes; what does not fit waits on the board
   for the next session or day. Nothing is marked by hand.
2. Order: deadline work first, then „Wichtig“ (`is_important`), then the board order.
3. Inside a session the next pick stays a To-do until the 25 minutes are used. The engine's existing five minute rule
   (done with ≥ 5 min left starts the next task, < 5 min left starts the break) is what ends it.
4. The card does not depend on the battery or on being inside a block; the area filter of a running block still applies.
5. A To-do that is bigger than 25 minutes (Short = 25 fits; Long = 75 does not) goes in alone.

## Decisions I made (please check)

- **Several sessions a day:** the card always shows the *next* session. When it is done the next bundle forms from what
  is left; I did not build a "three sessions today" plan view.
- **Battery:** the bundle is chosen at Okay whatever tonight's battery is. Low's warm-up and shrink still apply to the
  single suggestion above the lists.
- **Steps of projects and groups** stay in their own cards and are not part of the To-do session.
- **While a session runs** the folded board still shows the card (inert); its Start only exists in board mode.
- **`FUNCTIONS.md`** is listed in CLAUDE.md but does not exist in the repo; this note is the documentation for now.
