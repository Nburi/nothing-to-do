# Step 2.3 · Capture, Inbox and the daily sort

Refactor plan step 2.3 (R3; risks F8, C10). Built 2026-11-09 on `feature/capture-sort`, stacked on 2.2
(`feature/app-shell`).

## What changed

| Piece | What |
|---|---|
| Capture line | One line on every page of the new app (CONVENTIONS 5). Desktop: the field in the top bar, **C** opens it anywhere (not while typing or in a dialog). Mobile: **+** above the tab bar opens a sheet with one line above the keyboard. In a session: + in the rail (desktop) and in the drawer (mobile). After Enter the line empties and stays open for the next thought; Esc closes it. |
| `ShellCaptureController` | `POST /board/capture` and `DELETE /board/capture/{id}` (undo), JSON, same `Capture` service as the API. JSON instead of a Livewire call, so a dropped connection keeps the typed text with "Gerade keine Verbindung. Dein Text bleibt im Feld." and a retry, and a re-render never eats what is being typed. Undo works only on an untouched Inbox item of the user, up to 10 minutes after capture. 60 requests a minute. |
| Toast | "In der Inbox." with **Rückgängig** after every capture; "Zurückgenommen." after undo. |
| `App\Services\Planning\InboxSort` | The sort: To-do, Task or Later, size, hard and soft date, area, first step. A missing size is written as Short for To-dos and Tasks (G1); Later asks for no size and never keeps a hard date (it keeps a soft one, R8 table). The item goes to the end of its new list. Writes through `TaskMutator`. German messages per field. Undo restores every field it changed. |
| `App\Livewire\Shell\InboxSort` | The sort card: one item at a time over the board, progress bar, **1 / 2 / 3** pick the list, **Enter** places, **Esc** closes, "Später sortieren" sends the item to the back of the round, a toast with **Rückgängig** after every placement. Dates, area and first step fold away ("+ Termin, Bereich, erster Schritt"). Inline messages under each field; a soft date after the hard date gets a hint, not an error. Same card on both layouts (a bottom sheet on the phone). |
| Phone: the sort deck | **Niels' option A (2026-11-09)**, preview https://claude.ai/artifact/MedijEH3YqdbsCKo9G7YvD. Below 720 px the sort is a full-screen deck: the top Inbox card with "Wie lange?" (Short preselected) and "+ Termin, Bereich, erster Schritt", two cards behind it, and three piles at the bottom (To-dos, Tasks, Später, with counts). Drag the card onto a pile: it follows the finger 1:1, shrinks to half around the finger with a stamp ("TASKS") once it is over a pile, and flies onto it on release; anywhere else it springs back. Tapping a pile throws the card the same way. With the details open the card scrolls and drags by its grip. Under reduced motion it does not fly. Alpine island `sortDeck` (app.js), server side `InboxSort::placeTo()`. On Später the size chip is ignored and a hard date is dropped (the card says so under "Muss bis"). |
| Entry points | "Sortieren (n)" in the Inbox column (Werkbank board) and at the top of the Inbox tab of the list sheet / peek panel (Stapel, and both layouts in a session). |
| `App\Livewire\Shell\CaptureTokens` | Settings card "Kurzbefehl fürs Erfassen": name the token (iPhone, iPad), create it, see it **once** with Copy and the three Shortcut steps with the real URL, list with created / last used, **Widerrufen** with one inline "really?" (a revoked token cannot come back, so this is the one confirm). Only capture tokens are listed; full API tokens and other users' tokens are not. At most 10. `php artisan app:capture-token` still works. |
| Registry | `capture` and `sort` are available now; A11 requires them in both layouts and both modes. |
| `bootstrap/app.php` | Errors under `board/capture*` render as JSON, like `api/*`. |
| Copy | `lang/{de,en}/capture.php` (line and token card), `lang/{de,en}/sort.php`. |

## Fixed on the way (found in the browser)

- After a placement the board re-rendered and mounted the sort card afresh, so the card and its toast vanished.
  The card now has a stable key.
- Focus stayed on the "Sortieren" button behind the card, so Enter reopened the round instead of placing.
  The card takes focus when it opens.
- "Start · 10 min" wrapped onto two lines in the Stapel tray. One line now.
- The top bar lost "Board" as the active page after any Livewire update (it asked the route, which is the
  Livewire endpoint during an update). The page now passes it in.

## Decisions I made (please check)

The first three come from the phase 2 preview (https://claude.ai/artifact/U14VQTdSpG89XANKKEXT6v) and were
**confirmed by Niels on 2026-11-09**, together with the theme cards, the System/Hell/Dunkel switch, "Woche" → old
week plan and settings behind the gear.

- **The capture line stays open after Enter.**
- **Sorting is one card at a time**, not inline in the Inbox column.
- **Later asks less**: no size, no hard date; "Wo anfangen?" instead of "Erster Schritt".
- **Undo of a capture deletes the row** (it is seconds old and untouched). Everything else stays undoable instead
  of deleted.
- **Areas are chosen, not created, in the sort card.** Creating areas is part of the week plan editor (4.1); the
  card shows the chips only when there are areas.

## Not part of this step

- Voice capture and the offline queue (5.3). Today an offline capture keeps the text in the field; it is not
  queued.
- Swipe on the board's own lists (moving a card between columns or piles outside the sort). That comes with 3.1.
