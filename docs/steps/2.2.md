## The signature moment (CONVENTIONS 10, risk C5)

Not animated yet (that is 3.1), but the structure is ready for it: the shell root carries
`data-shell-mode="board|session"`, the board and the session are sibling regions (`data-region`) inside each
layout root, and every moving part outside them (timer, sheets, peek) is an Alpine island. Step 3.1 can render
both regions during the switch and fold one into the other without fighting Livewire's morphing.

## Decisions I made (please check)

- **Werkbank uses 2 columns between 720 and 1024 px.** Four columns at 760 px broke words mid-word.
- **"Woche" opens the old week plan** (marked *alt*) until 4.1; the board already reads what you enter there.
  The "Ideen" nav item stays hidden until the Later view (3.4).
- **Settings behind the gear** on both layouts; the four bottom tabs stay as planned.
- **Start buttons in the lists are disabled during work**, with "Erst nach dieser Session" (the engine does not
  switch tasks in a running session). In a break they start the next session with that task.
- **The ring fills with elapsed time**, as in the plan artifact.
- **No link from the old app yet.** Open `/board` directly during the beta. The old header is crowded with its own
  logic; I did not want to touch it for a link that lives two phases.
